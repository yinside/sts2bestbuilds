# Ironclad Strike Dummy Build Guide – Stack Strikes, Win Runs

This build revolves around the **Strike Dummy** relic and the new fishing rod that upgrades a random card every 3 normal combats. By stacking multiple copies of **Perfected Strike** and **Pommel Strike**, you scale damage rapidly while the free upgrades turn your deck into a powerhouse.

## Core Cards/Items

- **Perfected Strike** – Your main damage source. Grab every copy you see.
- **Pommel Strike** – Provides draw and is another Strike for scaling.
- **Twin Strike** – Solid early damage, also counts as a Strike.
- **Feed** – Scales max HP and is also a Strike. High priority.
- **Hunt** – Not a Strike, but offers card draw and weak. Take if you need utility.
- **Cruelty** – Vulnerable application; great with high damage.
- **Bash** – Innate vulnerable. Essential for burst turns.

## Best Relics/Equipment

- **Strike Dummy** – The cornerstone. +1 damage per Strike in deck (including itself).
- **Fishing Rod** – Free upgrades every 3 normal combats. Pairs perfectly with Strike Dummy.
- **Unceasing Top** – Extra card draw after playing all cards; helps cycle into Strikes.
- **Anchor** – Buffer damage on turn 1, especially useful in Act 1.
- **Shuriken** – Scales strength on 3-attack turns; works well with multi-hit Strikes.
- **Nunchaku** – Energy generation after 3 attacks; enables more Strikes per turn.

## Early Game Strategy

1. **Prioritize Strikes** – Take any Perfected Strike, Pommel Strike, or Twin Strike. Even basic Strikes are acceptable for the Dummy.
2. **Upgrade Bash** – Vulnerable is key. Upgrade it as soon as possible.
3. **Fish for Upgrades** – Use the fishing rod to upgrade your Strikes. Aim for at least 3-4 upgrades by the end of Act 1.
4. **Remove Defends** – You want to draw Strikes quickly. Remove Defends at shops.
5. **Take Feed** – If offered, take it. The HP scaling is invaluable, and it's a Strike.

## Mid Game Strategy

1. **Stack Strikes** – Continue adding Perfected Strikes. Your damage per card increases with each addition.
2. **Add Draw** – Pommel Strike is premium. If you lack draw, consider **Battle Trance** (but it's not a Strike, so be selective).
3. **Seek Vulnerable** – Keep Bash or add **Shockwave** or **Uppercut** for AoE vulnerable.
4. **Manage Deck Size** – Don't overfill with non-Strike cards. A 20-25 card deck is fine if most are Strikes.
5. **Upgrade Key Cards** – Upgrade Perfected Strikes and Feed first. Pommel Strike upgrades are nice but not critical.

## Late Game Scaling

1. **Infinite Potential** – With Unceasing Top and enough energy, you can cycle through your deck multiple times. Pommel Strikes help draw into more Strikes.
2. **Strength Scaling** – If you have **Shuriken** or **Vajra**, your Strikes hit even harder. **Limit Break** is a win condition if you have strength generation.
3. **Block Solutions** – Your damage is insane, but you still need block. **Flame Barrier** is decent; **Shrug It Off** is better. **Impervious** is premium.
4. **Final Bosses** – Against the Awakened One, avoid playing too many powers. Against the Time Eater, play carefully to not overdraw. Against Donu & Deca, focus one down quickly.

## Boss Matchups

- **Slime Boss** – Easy. Just hit hard and split when low. Your Strikes scale fast.
- **The Guardian** – Vulnerable is great. Bash + Strikes melts the shell.
- **Hexaghost** – You need block early. Use Flame Barrier or Shrug It Off. Your damage will outpace the burn.
- **Champ** – Wait until he buffs, then unleash a full hand of Strikes. Vulnerable helps.
- **Awakened One** – Avoid playing powers (like Limit Break) too early. Stick to Strikes and block.
- **Time Eater** – Don't play more than 12 cards per turn. Plan your turns carefully.
- **Donu & Deca** – Kill Donu first. Your single-target damage is high; focus fire.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Overvaluing non-Strike attacks** – Cards like **Whirlwind** or **Immolate** are good but dilute your Strike synergy. Only take them if you lack AoE.
2. **Ignoring block** – You can't out-damage everything. At least 3-4 block cards are necessary.
3. **Taking too many cards** – A 30+ card deck reduces consistency. Be disciplined.
4. **Skipping Feed** – It's a Strike and HP scaling. Almost always take it.
5. **Not upgrading Bash** – Vulnerable doubles your damage. Upgrade it early.

## Final Tips

- **Synergize with relics** – The **Fishing Rod** gives free upgrades, so prioritize upgrading Bash and Feed first. The **Strike Dummy** makes every Strike better.
- **Use the shop** – Buy **Strike Dummy** if you don't have it. Remove Defends. Look for **Pommel Strike** and **Feed**.
- **Pivot if needed** – If you don't get Strike Dummy by Act 2, consider transitioning to a different build. This build is heavily relic-dependent.
- **Play aggressively** – Your damage is high early. Take fights for upgrades and gold. But don't be reckless with HP.
- **Have fun** – Watching Perfected Strikes hit for 50+ damage each is satisfying. Enjoy the carnage.
