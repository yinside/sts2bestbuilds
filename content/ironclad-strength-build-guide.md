# Ironclad Strength Build Guide

## Quick Overview
This build focuses on stacking Strength through cards like Demon Form and Limit Break, then delivering massive damage with Heavy Blade. Prioritize scaling Strength and removing weak cards for a streamlined, high-damage deck.

## Core Cards/Items
- **Demon Form**: Your primary Strength scaling engine. Play early in long fights.
- **Limit Break**: Doubles your Strength. Essential for exponential scaling.
- **Heavy Blade**: The main damage dealer; benefits hugely from Strength.
- **Strength potions/events**: Any source of temporary or permanent Strength.
- **Card removal**: Strip Strikes to draw key cards faster.

## Best Relics/Equipment
- **Brimstone**: Gives 2 Strength per turn but enemies also gain Strength. Synergizes with scaling.
- **Red Skull**: +3 Strength when below 50% HP. Great for aggressive play.
- **Paper Phrog**: Enemies take 50% more damage when Vulnerable. Pairs with Heavy Blade.
- **Akabeko**: First attack each combat deals +8 damage. Excellent early burst.
- **Vajra**: +1 Strength at start of combat. Simple and effective.

## Early Game Strategy
- **Remove Strikes** as soon as possible. They dilute your deck.
- **Look for early damage**: Pommel Strike, Cleave, or Twin Strike to survive Act 1.
- **Prioritize campfires for upgrades** – upgrade Demon Form or Heavy Blade first.
- **Avoid taking too many cards**; keep your deck lean (10-15 cards by Act 1 boss).
- **Elites**: Fight early elites for relics, but only if your deck can handle them.

## Mid Game Strategy
- **Acquire Limit Break** – it’s the key to exponential scaling. Remove it if not upgraded yet.
- **Add block cards** like Shrug It Off or Power Through to survive while you set up.
- **Look for card draw**: Battle Trance or Offering to find your key pieces faster.
- **Consider second scaling option** like Spot Weakness for additional Strength.
- **Manage your HP** – use Rest sites if needed, but prioritize upgrades.

## Late Game Scaling
- **Stack Strength to 20+** using Demon Form + Limit Break combos.
- **One-shot enemies** with a single Heavy Blade (often 100+ damage).
- **Use Vulnerable** (from Bash or Paper Phrog) to multiply damage further.
- **Defend minimally** – your goal is to kill before you take too much damage.
- **If you have Brimstone**, be careful: enemies also scale, so you must kill fast.

## Boss Matchups
- **Hexaghost**: Play Demon Form early; its multi-hit attacks can be dangerous. Keep HP high.
- **Slime Boss**: Save Limit Break for after splitting to clean up slimes quickly.
- **Automaton**: Vulnerable is key; Hyper Beam can one-shot you, so kill before then.
- **Time Eater**: Limit Break is risky (costs 2 energy, uses a turn). Use Demon Form early and time your attacks carefully.
- **Awakened One**: Avoid playing too many Powers early; focus on scaling with Limit Break after it buffs.
- **Donu & Deca**: Kill Donu first (the one that gives Strength). Use AoE if possible.
- **Heart**: You need high block or a way to survive the big hits. Consider Barricade or Entrench if available.

## Common Mistakes
- **Taking too many cards**: Dilutes your core combo. Stick to essentials.
- **Neglecting block**: Even an aggressive build needs some defense to survive setup.
- **Playing Demon Form too late**: In long fights, play it as early as possible.
- **Over-relying on Limit Break without draw**: You need to draw it consistently; add card draw.
- **Ignoring enemy scaling**: With Brimstone, enemies get stronger; don’t stall.

## Final Tips
- **Upgrade Limit Break** to make it cost 0 energy – it’s a priority.
- **Keep a copy of Bash** for Vulnerable; don’t remove it.
- **Use potions** wisely: Strength potion early, block potion for emergencies.
- **Flex** is a trap – temporary Strength isn’t worth a card slot unless you have artifact removal.
- **Practice the combo**: Demon Form -> Limit Break -> Heavy Blade. Master the timing.

Now go crush the Spire with raw power!
