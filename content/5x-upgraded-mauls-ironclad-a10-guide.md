# 5x Upgraded Mauls Go Absolutely Insane | Ironclad A10 Guide

## Quick Overview
This build revolves around stacking multiple upgraded Mauls (or similar multi-hit attacks like Bludgeon, Stampede, and Tremble) to overwhelm enemies with massive damage output. The key is to leverage Headbutt to replay your big hitters, Dominate for Strength and Vulnerable, and potions to survive early elites. By mid-game, you'll be one-shotting elites and cruising through Act 2.

## Core Cards/Items
- **Bludgeon**: Your primary damage dealer. Upgrade ASAP for 63 base damage.
- **Headbutt**: Replays Bludgeon (or any key card) from discard pile. Essential for consistent high damage.
- **Dominate**: Provides 2 Vulnerable and 2 Strength on one card. Pairs perfectly with Bludgeon.
- **Stampede**: Reduces the cost of your next attack to 0. Combo with Headbutt for free Bludgeons.
- **Tremble**: Multi-hit attack that scales with Strength. Great for AoE and synergizes with Dominate.
- **Energy Potion**: Enables early combos like Bludgeon + Headbutt + Bludgeon.
- **Vulnerable Potion**: Guarantees Vulnerable for burst turns.

## Best Relics/Equipment
- **Tuning Fork**: Generates block when you play attacks. Excellent for survivability while going all-in.
- **Stone Armor**: Gives 6 Plating (block each turn). Upgrades well and lets you focus on damage.
- **Potion Slot**: Extra potion capacity is huge for this build—more Energy Potions or Vulnerable Potions.
- **Drum of Battle**: Draws a card on turn 1. Helps find your key pieces.

## Early Game Strategy
- **Path aggressively**: Prioritize combats and elites to build your deck quickly. Use potions to survive elite fights.
- **Pick Bludgeon** if offered—it solves Act 1 damage. Upgrade it at the first campfire.
- **Take Headbutt** early to recycle Bludgeon. A single Headbutt lets you deal 63 damage every other turn.
- **Grab Dominate** for Vulnerable and Strength. Two Dominates stack beautifully.
- **Use potions liberally** in Act 1 elites. An Energy Potion lets you Bludgeon + Headbutt + Bludgeon for 126 damage.

## Mid Game Strategy
- **Transition to multi-hit attacks**: Add Tremble and Stampede. Stampede makes your next attack free—combo with Headbutt for infinite loops.
- **Upgrade priority**: Bludgeon > Dominate > Stampede > Headbutt.
- **Manage deck size**: Keep it lean (15-20 cards) to draw your combo pieces consistently. Remove Strikes at shops.
- **Use Headbutt to set up**: Put Bludgeon on top of your draw pile, then next turn play it with Stampede for free.
- **Survive with block**: Stone Armor and Tuning Fork provide enough defense. Don't take too many block cards—damage is your best defense.

## Late Game Scaling
- **Stack Strength**: Dominate and Tremble scale with Strength. If you find additional Strength relics (e.g., Vajra), grab them.
- **Infinite potential**: With multiple Headbutts and Stampede, you can loop attacks indefinitely—Headbutt a Stampede, play a free Bludgeon, Headbutt it again.
- **AoE clear**: Tremble handles multiple enemies. Upgrade it for extra hits.
- **Potion insurance**: Keep an Energy Potion and Vulnerable Potion for boss fights. They enable 200+ damage turns.

## Boss Matchups
- **Phantom (Act 1)**: Multi-hit attacks wreck this boss. Use Bludgeon + Headbutt to kill quickly. Block only when necessary.
- **Slime Boss**: AoE from Tremble is key. Save Headbutt to replay Tremble if needed.
- **Guardian**: Vulnerable is essential. Dominate + Bludgeon destroys the Guardian's armor.
- **Champ (Act 2)**: Burst him down before he buffs. Use all potions to kill in 2-3 turns.
- **Awakened One (Act 3)**: Avoid playing too many Powers. Stick to attacks and scale Strength with Dominate.

## Common Mistakes
- **Overvaluing block**: This build is about damage racing. Taking too many block cards dilutes your damage output.
- **Not upgrading Bludgeon first**: Bludgeon's upgrade doubles its damage. Always upgrade it at the first campfire.
- **Misusing Headbutt**: Don't Headbutt a weak card. Always set up your biggest hitter.
- **Saving potions too long**: Use them to survive elites early. Hoarding potions leads to death.
- **Ignoring Vulnerable**: Without Vulnerable, your damage is halved. Dominate and Vulnerable Potion are critical.

## Final Tips
- **Embrace variance**: Sometimes you draw all your Headbutts before Bludgeon. That's okay—the build is consistent enough.
- **Watch your deck order**: Use Headbutt to control what you draw next turn. Plan 2-3 turns ahead.
- **Experiment with duplicates**: Two Dominates or two Headbutts are never bad. They increase consistency.
- **Be aggressive**: This build rewards high-risk, high-reward plays. Take elites whenever possible.
- **Have fun**: Nothing beats the satisfaction of one-shotting an elite with a 5x upgraded Maul. Enjoy the carnage!
