# How To Win As Regent In Slay The Spire 2 | Top Player Guide

## Quick Overview
The Regent is a star-focused character that generates and spends stars for powerful effects. Success hinges on efficient star generation, strong payoffs, and careful deck manipulation to avoid draw order dependency. Prioritize combo-oriented decks over generic good-stuff piles.

## Core Cards/Items
- **Glow (A-tier)**: 1 energy, gain 1 star, draw 2 cards. Upgrade to gain 2 stars. Excellent efficiency and enables combos.
- **Royal Gamble (A-tier)**: 0 energy, 5 stars → gain 9 stars, exhaust. Upgrade to retain. Solves star generation for a fight.
- **Genesis (A-tier)**: 2 energy power, gain 2 stars at start of turn. Dominates long fights.
- **Hidden Cash (A-tier)**: 1 energy, gain 1 star now, 3 next turn (total 4 stars). Efficient but don't overpick.
- **Gathering Light (A-tier)**: Gain 7 block and 1 star. Great early defense that scales.
- **Solar Strike (A-tier)**: 1 energy, deal 8 damage, gain 1 star. Upgrade adds another star. Solid common attack.
- **Shining Strike (B-tier)**: 1 energy, deal 8 damage, gain 2 stars, then put on top of draw pile. Fine early but clogs draws later.
- **Knuckle Blow (B-tier)**: 3 energy, deal 30 damage. If kills enemy, gain 5 stars. Good early, falls off late.
- **Alignment (Key Payoff)**: 0 energy, 2 stars → gain 2 energy (3 upgraded). Infinite combo enabler.
- **Falling Star (Starter Payoff)**: 0 energy, apply Vulnerable, deal 7 damage. Strong early but draw-dependent.

## Best Relics/Equipment
- **Starter Relic**: Gives 3 stars at start of combat – enables turn 1 Falling Star.
- **Star-Generation Relics**: Any relic that grants stars at turn start or on specific triggers (e.g., "Gain 1 star when you play a skill") is high priority.
- **Energy Relics**: Since Alignment converts stars to energy, relics that give energy (e.g., Happy Flower, Nuclear Battery) help sustain loops.
- **Draw Relics**: Bag of Preparation, Gambling Chip, or any relic that improves draw consistency.
- **Exhaust Synergy**: Cards like Royal Gamble exhaust; relics that trigger on exhaust (e.g., Charon's Ashes) add value.

## Early Game Strategy
- **Focus on star generation and basic payoffs.** Pick up Glow, Solar Strike, or Gathering Light to stabilize.
- **Use Falling Star aggressively** for Vulnerable and damage. Try to pair it with another attack for maximum value.
- **Avoid oversaturating with star generation.** 2-3 generators are enough; you need payoffs to spend them.
- **Prioritize card draw** (e.g., Glow) to reduce draw order issues.
- **Take damage cards** like Shining Strike or Knuckle Blow only if you lack early damage for elites.

## Mid Game Strategy
- **Transition to combo pieces.** Look for Alignment and additional star payoffs (e.g., cards that deal damage or block based on stars).
- **Add deck manipulation** (e.g., card draw, retain) to consistently line up star generators with payoffs.
- **Remove starter cards** (especially Venerate) to improve deck consistency.
- **Evaluate star generation vs. payoff balance.** If you have Royal Gamble, you may not need Hidden Cash.
- **Start planning for scaling** – powers like Genesis become crucial for long fights.

## Late Game Scaling
- **Infinite combos** are the strongest win condition. Use Glow (upgraded) to generate 2 stars and draw, plus Alignment to convert stars to energy, looping indefinitely.
- **Star generation engines** like Genesis + Royal Gamble provide massive star output each turn, enabling repeated use of high-impact payoffs.
- **Stack block and damage** through star-spending cards that scale (e.g., a card that deals damage equal to stars spent).
- **Exhaust unnecessary cards** to reduce deck size and increase consistency.
- **Use retain** on key cards (e.g., upgraded Royal Gamble) to save them for the right moment.

## Boss Matchups
- **Hexaghost**: High damage output early. Prioritize block (Gathering Light) and try to kill before second big attack. Star generation helps outpace.
- **Slime Boss**: Split mechanic can be dangerous if you rely on single-target. AoE star payoffs (if available) or multiple Falling Stars help.
- **The Guardian**: Defensive mode requires burst damage. Save star payoffs for when it's vulnerable. Use Genesis for sustained star income.
- **Time Eater**: Limit card plays to 12 per turn. Focus on high-impact star payoffs (e.g., upgraded Alignment loops) rather than many small cards.
- **Awakened One**: Avoid playing too many powers early (it gains Strength). Use non-power star generation like Glow. Save powers for after the first phase.
- **The Heart**: Needs high block and damage. Infinite combos or massive star generation + block payoffs are key. Vulnerable from Falling Star helps.

## Common Mistakes
- **Over-drafting star generation** without enough payoffs – you'll float stars and lose tempo.
- **Ignoring draw and deck manipulation** – Regent is draw-order dependent; without it, you'll brick frequently.
- **Taking too many expensive cards** – Regent's best cards are cheap (0-1 energy). High-cost cards like Knuckle Blow can clog hands.
- **Forgetting to exhaust starter cards** – Venerate becomes dead weight once you have better generators.
- **Not planning for scaling** – relying on early damage falls off in Act 3; you need a combo or power-based win condition.

## Final Tips
- **Glow is the best common card** – prioritize it heavily for its star generation and draw.
- **Test Alignment combos** in safe fights to understand loop timing.
- **Balance your deck** around a single strong synergy rather than mixing multiple strategies.
- **Use the starter relic's 3 stars** to play Falling Star on turn 1 for early damage and debuff.
- **Watch your star count** – don't waste stars on weak effects when you could save for a game-changing payoff.

Master these strategies, and you'll be crushing max Ascensions with the Regent in no time!
